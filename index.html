Introduction
Simulation

This JavaScript program simulates a Yes/No task in which the (simulated) participants are presented with stimuli of differing intensties and are required to responed, well, either Yes or No to if they detected to stimulus or not.

A simple psychometric function, Ψ, is used for generating the simulated responses and for statistical inference.

Ψ(s; theta = {c, σ}) = c + s/σ
P(s; Yes) = Φ(Ψ(s, theta))

This function has two parameters, one corresponding the the criterion or the propensity of the participant to respond Yes and the other, sigma (σ) corresponding to noise in the detection system. The latter is related to the detection threshold. The function Φ is the cumulative distribution function of normal distribution.

You can select three different stimulus selection schemes. On selects random stimuli, one places stimuli at the posterior mean of the threshold estimate and one attempts to minimise posterior entropy.
